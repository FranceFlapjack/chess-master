Chapter 2 · The knight

A knight on the rim is dim.

Every square here is coloured by how many squares a knight standing on it can jump to. Two from a corner. Eight from the middle. That is why good players bring their knights to the centre.

Chapter 5 · The story so far

Fourteen centuries, five moments.

  1. c. 600India: chaturanga, the game that became chess.
  2. Late 1400sEurope: the queen gets her long, sweeping move.
  3. 1858Paris: Paul Morphy plays the Opera Game, at the opera.
  4. 1997New York: the computer Deep Blue beats world champion Garry Kasparov.
  5. TodayYou. Your first lesson takes five minutes.
